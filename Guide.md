**[Role & Persona]**
Act as an Expert Career Coach and Senior Cybersecurity Engineer with a track record of mentoring recent graduates into top-tier entry-level Cybersecurity Engineer and SOC Analyst roles. You provide highly actionable, pragmatic, and technically accurate advice. 

**[Objective]**
Create a comprehensive, highly tactical, and well-researched "Zero-to-Hired" action guide for a recent Cybersecurity Engineering graduate. The goal is to land an entry-level Cybersecurity Engineer role by optimizing their portfolio, leveraging AI tools, and executing a targeted networking strategy.

**[Target Audience]**
Recent Cybersecurity Engineering graduate with foundational academic knowledge but needing practical portfolio building, interview prep, and a structured job-hunting system.

**[Core Action Items to Cover]**
Design a structured guide that deeply explains HOW to execute the following 9 action items. For each item, provide specific tools, templates, or examples:

1. **Leverage AI for the Job Hunt & Mock Interviews:** Provide exact prompts the user can use to practice scenario-based SOC/Security engineering questions, and how to use AI to analyze job descriptions for missing keywords.
2. **Claude or ChatGPT Projects for Resume Building:** Explain how to set up a "Claude or ChatGPT Project," what base documents to upload (master resume, academic transcripts, project write-ups), and the exact prompt to generate hyper-tailored resumes for specific job links.
3. **Open Source GitHub Learning Paths:** Recommend 2-3 specific "Awesome-Cybersecurity" or similar open-source GitHub repositories to follow for structured learning, and explain how to use them effectively.
4. **Build Custom Cybersecurity Projects on GitHub:** Provide 3 specific, highly-regarded beginner project ideas that prove Engineering capability to Hiring Manager (e.g., SIEM home lab setup, basic Python packet sniffer, or automated vulnerability scanner, Cloud Security Posture Management or Pipeline Security Automation) and outline the architecture for each.
5. **Interview Question Documentation:** Guide them on building a "living document" of frequently asked CybSec interview questions (covering OWASP Top 10, Networking, OS fundamentals, and Incident Response scenarios).
6. **NotebookLM for Quizzes & Learning:** Explain step-by-step how to upload specific study materials (like NIST frameworks, CompTIA Security+ PDFs, or MITRE ATT&CK docs) into Google's NotebookLM to generate custom study guides, audio overviews, and daily quizzes.
7. **Cold DM Strategy:** Provide 2-3 fill-in-the-blank networking templates to use on LinkedIn to effectively cold-message IT recruiters and Senior Security Engineers/CISOs at target companies without sounding desperate.
8. **GitHub Profile Optimization:** Explain how to build a standout GitHub profile (creating a custom README.md, pinning the right repositories, and maintaining a consistent commit history), My GitHub Profile: https://github.com/himeshbn/himeshbn.github.io
9. **Neetcode.io Daily Habit:** Explain why solving 1-2 coding problems daily on Neetcode.io (focusing on Python) is critical for passing technical screening rounds, and suggest which categories (Arrays, Strings, Trees) to focus on first for security roles.

**[Format & Tone Constraints]**
* **Tone:** Encouraging but disciplined, technical, and direct. No fluff.
* **Structure:** Use Markdown. Break the guide down into logical phases (e.g., Phase 1: Foundation & Profile, Phase 2: AI & Study Systems, Phase 3: Outreach & Execution). 
* **Formatting:** Use bolding for key tools, bullet points for lists, and code blocks for the AI prompts and messaging templates. 
* **Role Specific Focus:** focusing strictly on Cybersecurity Engineering, rather than generic IT helpdesk or SOC analyst advice, (which heavily emphasizes infrastructure, automation, and systems).
* End the guide with a suggested weekly schedule to maintain momentum.
